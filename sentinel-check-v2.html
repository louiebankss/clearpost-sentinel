Safer next step

Don't use contact details or payment links in a suspicious message. Open the official app or type a known website yourself. Verify unexpected requests through a previously trusted contact method.

What this scanner checks

Package-fee requests, suspicious job offers, payment-detail changes, account-recovery impersonation, pressure tactics, sensitive-information requests, prize claims, shortened links, HTTP links, and numeric-IP links.

These are clues, not proof. Legitimate messages can trigger warnings, and scams can evade detection.

Privacy: this demo analyzes the text in your browser and does not intentionally transmit it to a server. It does not open or verify links, inspect other apps, or replace professional security tools.
