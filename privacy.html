Privacy comes first.

ClearPost Sentinel is an early-stage digital safety project designed to help people recognize potential scams and suspicious messages.

How message checking works

The Sentinel Check V2 prototype analyzes the text you enter using rules that run in your browser. The scanner is designed to analyze that text locally rather than send it to a server.

Do not enter passwords, verification codes, banking details, or other sensitive personal information.

What the prototype does not do

  • It does not monitor your other apps or messages.
  • It does not independently verify a sender or website.
  • It does not guarantee that a message is safe or fraudulent.
  • It does not provide antivirus protection.
