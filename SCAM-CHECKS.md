
# ClearPost Sentinel — Scam Detection Rules

## Purpose
Document the scam patterns Sentinel Check is designed to flag.

## 1. Fake delivery fees
**Signals:** Unexpected delivery charges, redelivery fees, or customs fees requested through a message.

**Safe action:** Check tracking through the carrier's official app or website.

## 2. Job and employment scams
**Signals:** Upfront training fees, requests to return part of a check, or demands to purchase equipment through a specific payment method.

**Safe action:** Verify the employer using independently sourced contact details.

## 3. Payment redirection
**Signals:** Unexpected changes to bank details, payroll information, wire instructions, or payment accounts.

**Safe action:** Confirm the change using a previously trusted phone number.

## 4. Account recovery impersonation
**Signals:** Someone claiming to be support who requests passwords, verification codes, or remote access.

**Safe action:** Never share sign-in codes. Contact the service through its official app or website.

## Important limitations
These rules are indicators, not proof of fraud. A message without flagged indicators is not guaranteed safe. Detection should be tested against both scam examples and legitimate messages.

## Privacy principle
Scanned messages should remain in the user's browser unless a future feature clearly explains and obtains consent for any data transmission.
